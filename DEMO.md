# After Hours: demo video shot list (about 75 seconds)

Record in a desktop browser at 1280px wide, full screen, on the live site. Turn on dark mode and hide bookmarks.
Best time to record: Sunday night or before Monday's open (Wall Street closed), so the verdicts are dramatic. If you record during market hours, open the history chart to show the weekend instead (shot 6).

| # | Time | On screen | Do | Say (voice-over or caption) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–8s | Home page, top | Hold on the headline and the "Wall Street is closed · opens in …" pill | "Wall Street is closed. But on Robinhood Chain, its stocks still trade, and 59% of that trading happens after the bell." |
| 2 | 8–18s | Tonight's board | Scroll slowly down the board | "When the market's closed, the pools get thin, and nobody's checking the price against the real stock. This board does it for every stock token, live." |
| 3 | 18–30s | Check form → NVDA result | Pick NVDA, Sell, $10k, press **Check this trade**. Land on the red "Wait for the open" card | "Say I want to sell ten thousand dollars of Nvidia right now. After Hours quotes my exact trade onchain and compares it with Chainlink's last real price. I'd lose about eighty dollars. Its call: wait for the open." |
| 4 | 30–42s | The numbers, the breakdown, the routes | Point (cursor) at drift vs size impact, then "Where it fills best", then the official-token check | "It shows where the cost comes from: the pool has drifted, and my size moves it a little more. And it confirms this is Robinhood's real NVDA contract, not a copycat." |
| 5 | 42–55s | Protected sell | Leave it on 0.3%, press **Protected sell**. Show the red "Stopped. Nothing was sent." | "A normal swap only protects you against the market's own price. The single Uniswap pool would pay about a hundred dollars a share. The best route across seven venues pays about seven hundred eighty, and After Hours uses it." |
| 7 | 63–70s | Share card | Click **Download card** or show the X post preview | "Every check makes a share card, so you can warn your followers before they trade." |
| 8 | 70–75s | Home page headline | Hold | "After Hours. Check the trade before you make it. Built with Claude Code." |

## Before recording
- Open each page once first, so it's warm and loads instantly.
- Numbers change all the time. Say "about", and don't put exact figures in captions you prepare in advance.
- Don't connect a real wallet on camera. Shot 5 doesn't need one.
- Don't say "safe", "guaranteed", or give advice. Say "shows the cost" and "its call".

## Fallback
If Robinhood Chain is slow while you record, use the screenshots of the same pages and the share cards (`/api/og?symbol=NVDA&side=sell&usd=10000`) as stills, with the same voice-over.
